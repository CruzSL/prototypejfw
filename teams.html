How we play

Joga Bonito meets Tiki-Taka.

Every JFW squad learns one way of playing, so a player can move up and still feel at home.

01

Creativity and freedom

Joga Bonito. Players are free to try things, express themselves and enjoy the ball.

02

Possession and movement

Tiki-Taka. We keep the ball, move as one unit and create space together.

03

Positioning and decisions

Players learn where to stand and why, so they play with purpose and understanding.

One family

Two programs.

New · Open to join

JFW Academy

A structured development program launching with U10 and U12. More time to learn, grow and settle into the JFW way of playing.

  • U10 and U12 at launch
  • Built around the JFW methodology
  • Fees are shared before you commit
  • A long-term pathway into the JFW Club
Scouted · Competitive

JFW Club

Our competitive U16 squad, built by scouting and recruiting high-potential players, including talent from outside Jakarta.

  • Plays in major youth competitions
  • A U16 to U18 pathway
  • Scholarships come through scouting and trials, and families can also pay to join
  • Players are developed with the aim of reaching professional academies and teams
