How our coaches work

The same standards.
Every session.

01

One methodology

Every coach teaches the JFW way: Joga Bonito creativity with Tiki-Taka movement and decision-making.

02

Honest feedback

Families get honest feedback after every match, and a clear review each term.

03

Safe by design

Every coach is checked and trained in child safeguarding, and there is one named person you can call. Safeguarding contact: name to be announced.
