Questions parents ask

Straight answers.

Choosing an academy is a big call. Here is what we tell every family.

Is my child safe?

Every coach is checked and trained in child safeguarding, and there is one named person you can call.

How will I know how my child is doing?

You get honest feedback after every match, and a clear review each term.

Will my child actually play?

Minutes depend on effort and standards, and we explain them before you join.

What if I do not know their level?

Come to a trial. Our first trial session is Saturday, 12 December 2026. The coach places your child where they will grow best.

What does it cost?

Fees are shared before you commit. No surprises.

Can my child get a scholarship?

For the U16 squad, scholarships come through trials and scouting. Families can also pay to join the U16 squad.
