About

Our story.

Jakarta Football Warriors is a youth football club building a real football identity in Jakarta.

Who we are

Built on talent.
Now building a style.

Jakarta Football Warriors has spent the past few seasons building a genuine football identity in Jakarta. Our U16 and U18 teams compete against some of the country's most established academies.

We find promising young players and recruit them directly. Many of our players come from underprivileged regions outside Jakarta, and the club sponsors them fully. Families can also pay to join the U16 squad.

Now we are opening the JFW Academy, a new program for younger players, so more children can grow up inside the JFW way of playing.

One family

Two programs. One mission.

Building football in Jakarta the right way, and creating real opportunity for the next generation of Indonesian players.

Community

More than
a scoreboard.

01

Scholarship players

We scout talented young players from underprivileged regions outside Jakarta who would otherwise never get access to competitive football at this level. The club sponsors them fully.

02

JFW Charity Cup

In 2024 and 2025 we hosted a free football competition for underdeveloped teams, with PSSI, Kemenpora (the Ministry of Youth and Sports) and DPR RI (the House of Representatives).

03

Orphanage program

JFW coaches have shared football skills and training methods with children at SOS Children's Village, building the mindset and discipline to step onto a bigger stage.
